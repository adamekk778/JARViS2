JARVIS is a personal assistant that runs on your computer. This app is its phone:

• talk to it from across the flat or on the road
• notices, alarms and reminders from the PC on your phone and watch
• NFC tags, location sharing, document scanning, PC remote control
• offline commands when there is no connection

Your data stays on your computer. The app needs JARVIS on a Windows PC and pairing by QR code.
